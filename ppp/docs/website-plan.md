# Website specification — founder review

Prepared 7 October 2026. Recommendations and planning estimates are distinguished from verified source facts in `research.md`.

## Recommendation

Build a mobile-first Indian jewellery boutique around a deliberately small edit. Gold-plated silver is the principal online category. Lightweight real-gold pieces, mainly 18K with selected 22K designs, support everyday and impulse purchases. A festive line serves occasion purchases. Higher-value and bridal pieces use an assisted consultation route.

Online discovery can target India, with delivery limited to serviceable, insured pincodes. Local consultations focus on Erode, Tirupur, Coimbatore and Karur. Do not advertise guaranteed home service across every pincode in these cities before the parent company's team and suppliers confirm coverage.

The primary digital audience is women and men in their 20s through their mid-40s. The below-₹30,000 range should be easy to browse. The founder has not set festive price bands or the final assortment size.

## 1. Identity and theme

Recommended direction: quiet contemporary design with restrained Indian warmth.

| Element | Direction |
|---|---|
| Canvas | Warm ivory `#F6F3EC`, paper `#FFFCF6`, sand `#E8DFD0` |
| Text and emphasis | Ink `#292723`; plum `#482B3B`; antique gold `#96743C` |
| Typography | Expressive serif headlines, clear sans-serif descriptions and commerce controls |
| Photography | Accurate close-ups; scale on real people; a mix of women and men; natural skin tones |
| Styling | Everyday clothing and restrained festive settings; textiles and architectural references used selectively |
| Layout | Spacious editorial introductions followed by concise, usable product information |
| Motion | Subtle feedback; respect reduced-motion preferences |
| Voice | Warm, informed and precise about materials; explain curation without implying exclusive manufacture |

The offline preview uses system fonts and original SVG illustrations so it can be downloaded and opened without external services. Production typography and imagery will follow final identity approval and asset licensing.

`THE EDIT` is a temporary wordmark for review. The new brand name, logo and domain are still being developed. Introduce the parent brand through Our Story only. Use its verified history without inventing a founding year, location reach or supplier relationship.

## 2. Catalogue architecture

Organise discovery by use and collection. Keep material labels visible everywhere.

| Collection | Role |
|---|---|
| Everyday | Regular-wear pieces with the below-₹30,000 range prominent |
| Small Gold — working name | Lightweight real-gold creations; predominantly 18K, selected 22K |
| Festive | Occasion pieces with separate pricing to be decided |
| The Edit | A small curated selection, refreshed when actual stock and demand justify it |
| Private Appointments | Higher-value, wedding and bridal enquiry journey |

One product can appear in several collections while retaining one inventory record. Filters should include category, material, price, purity where relevant, and ready-stock/made-to-order status. Do not offer filter choices that contain no relevant products at launch.

Avoid using the ambiguous term "one-gram jewellery" as the sole product description. The founder has clarified that this category contains real gold. List 18K or 22K explicitly, and publish verified net metal weight for each SKU. Whether the approximate one-gram intention refers to alloy weight or pure-gold content remains to be confirmed from supplier specifications; never infer it from purity alone.

The visual prototype demonstrates four invented products. It does not recommend these exact designs, prices or stock levels. The budget assumes approximately 30 launch designs solely to size the work.

## 3. Sitemap and homepage

Primary navigation: Shop, The Edit, Private Appointments, Our Story. Search and bag are utility controls. Account access is optional for launch; guest checkout should remain straightforward.

Production pages:

1. Home.
2. Shop and collection pages.
3. Product detail template with material-specific fields.
4. Cart and hosted checkout.
5. Appointment explanation and request form.
6. Our Story.
7. Care and material guide.
8. Contact and order support.
9. Delivery, returns, warranty, privacy and terms pages.
10. Order confirmation and appointment acknowledgement.

Homepage sequence: editorial introduction → curated products → Small Gold story → festive selection → private appointments → Our Story invitation. Product and service pages carry relevant trust details beside the decision, not solely in the footer.

English is the launch language. Prepare translation fields from the start; Tamil is the recommended next language for the local consultation audience. Publish only reviewed translations. Additional languages should follow demonstrated demand.

## 4. Customer journeys

### Ready-stock purchase

Acquisition page → relevant collection → product → variant/quantity → cart → serviceable delivery address → final charges → online payment → confirmation → dispatch and delivery updates → care/support.

Stock must be decremented consistently across the website and parent-store operations. A displayed ready-stock promise must reflect stock that the business controls or has reserved.

### Made-to-order or supplier-sourced purchase

Product → explicit fulfilment status and lead time → confirmed specifications and fixed price → payment → milestone updates → dispatch.

If the supplier cannot confirm price or availability, use an enquiry/quote action instead of taking immediate payment. Quote validity and permissible cancellation/return terms must be clear before acceptance.

### WhatsApp assistance

Product → customer initiates assistance with product reference → staff answers → secure payment/checkout route. Marketing opt-in is separate from assistance or order notifications. Do not automatically subscribe customers to broadcasts.

### High-value consultation

Service page → name/contact/city/interests/budget/preferred timing → staff review → necessary verification → store/home eligibility decision → confirmed appointment → supplier/staff coordination → consultation → quote/payment/invoice and follow-up.

Store and home visits are distinct choices. A submitted enquiry is not a confirmed booking. Do not collect identity-document uploads on the initial public form. Establish any later verification process, retention and access rules with the responsible team.

## 5. Product-page specification

- Material displayed beside the title and price.
- Multiple photographs, on-body scale and an optional short wearing video.
- Purity, net metal weight, dimensions and stone details where relevant.
- Base metal and plating details for plated items; do not describe gold-plated silver as solid gold.
- Ready-stock, made-to-order or supplier-confirmation status.
- Actual price and valid variants; lead time and delivery eligibility.
- Applicable hallmark or supplier documentation described per item after validation.
- Care guidance and warranty scope supported by the supplier.
- Concise return eligibility and timing, linked to the complete policy.
- Online purchase and customer-initiated assistance where appropriate.

No unverified claims such as universally waterproof, hypoallergenic, lifetime plating, guaranteed investment returns or sitewide BIS certification. Claims require supporting specifications and applicable legal review.

## 6. Operations

No COD. Shortlist UPI/card checkout and a jewellery-specialist insured delivery arrangement. BVC is a research candidate, not an appointed supplier. Obtain written terms for accepted materials, value limits, serviceable pincodes, pickup requirements, custody, loss claims and reverse logistics before selection.

Free-delivery eligibility should be derived from actual contribution margin. Do not publish a rupee threshold until approved.

Returns: request inside a published window → eligibility decision → authorised return transport → receipt and documented inspection → outcome communication → refund if approved. Publish a defined inspection and refund turnaround; avoid an indefinite inspection condition. Distinguish change-of-mind requests from defects, incorrect items and applicable consumer obligations. Final rules require an Indian consumer-law review.

Admin data: SKU, supplier reference, material, purity, weight, images, cost, selling price, quantity, sourcing mode, lead time, documentation, return eligibility and appointment owner/status. Assign individual staff roles; do not rely on a shared owner login.

## 7. Platform decision

Start from a tailored Shopify theme because the intended workflow requires catalogue, orders and owner-operated commerce. Use standard platform functionality before commissioning custom services. This prototype is not a deployable Shopify theme.

Important commercial finding: current official pages list a 2% third-party-provider fee on Shopify Basic and standard Razorpay gateway pricing of 2% + GST. The two fees are separate. An approximate 4% combined headline cost before applicable fee taxes can be material for gold margins; billing bases, payment methods and merchant-specific terms must be confirmed. See the linked research and editable model.

Basic may suit an initial single-admin pilot. Grow lists up to five staff accounts and a lower 1% third-party-provider fee. Based on annual-billing subscription prices, the incremental subscription cost is offset at approximately ₹4.10 lakh/month of equally eligible transaction volume, before taxes and other differences. Staff access may independently justify the plan change.

Final gate: real product contribution margins, expected transaction volume, needed staff roles, merchant approval and provider terms. If total fees make the model unattractive, compare a managed WooCommerce option with an actual maintenance quote before committing; no verified WooCommerce cost comparison is included here.

## 8. Scope, cost and schedule

The budget spreadsheet allocates ₹1.45–₹3.20 lakh before contingency, or approximately ₹1.7–₹3.7 lakh with 15% contingency. Reserve ₹2.5–₹3 lakh provisionally. These are planning allowances for externally commissioned work, not market quotations or charges from the assistant. Implementing together may reduce contractor spending.

Exclusions: inventory, GST/taxes, advertising, exhibitions, transaction fees, shipping, insurance, returns, ongoing human support and unusual integrations. Avoid double-counting an agency's integration/testing work if already included in its development quote.

Software reserve: ₹3,000–₹10,000/month, subject to plan and app choices; this is an estimate, not a platform price. Verified subscription prices are listed separately in the research.

| Stage | Indicative duration | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| Research, requirements and operations decisions | 3–5 working days | Fees and product model are acceptable |
| Identity application and page design | 5–7 working days | Founder approves desktop/mobile design |
| Store implementation and integrations | 8–12 working days | Sandbox order and enquiry paths function |
| Catalogue, policies and launch checks | 4–6 working days | Real data, provider approvals and operations are ready |

Allow roughly 4–6 weeks with prompt decisions and available assets. These are estimates, not a delivery commitment. Supplier onboarding or brand photography may extend the schedule.

## 9. Launch acceptance criteria

- All published products have correct material, purity where relevant, price, supplier-backed specifications and images.
- Ready-stock orders and supplier-confirmation enquiries follow different paths correctly.
- UPI/card success, failure and duplicate-callback behaviour are tested in the chosen provider's supported test mode.
- Inventory and confirmation emails remain correct after successful and failed payments.
- COD is unavailable; delivery eligibility and final charges are clear before payment.
- Refund and return workflows are exercised with authorised test orders.
- Appointment enquiry reaches the responsible person without implying automatic home-visit approval.
- Keyboard navigation, labels, focus, mobile layout, readable contrast and reduced motion are checked.
- Analytics captures agreed commerce events without logging sensitive customer details.
- Policies, documentation claims, privacy and material-specific disclosures are approved before publication.

Optional later work: loyalty, complex product personalisation, automated WhatsApp campaigns, advanced CRM, live metal-rate pricing, full catalogue translation and designer-drop infrastructure. These are not part of the initial prototype.

Then confirm the brand name, real launch catalogue, precise weight conventions, cost/margin sheet, fulfilment promises, return terms, free-delivery threshold, staff roles, launch date and platform spend. These decisions do not prevent concept review; they do prevent a truthful production launch.
