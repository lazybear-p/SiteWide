# Decision record

## Confirmed by the founder

- Use SiteWide for the project and provide downloadable files. The earlier local-Desktop-only preference is superseded.
- Gold-plated silver is the principal online category in the original brief.
- The approximately one-gram concept contains real gold; mostly 18K with some 22K designs.
- Everyday purchases below ₹30,000 and a festive line are important.
- Online audience: women and men in their 20s to mid-40s; national targeting.
- Parent business is in Erode. High-value/home-consultation focus: Erode, Tirupur, Coimbatore, Karur.
- Parent-brand endorsement belongs in Our Story.
- Mix ready stock and made-to-order/supplier-sourced products.
- Existing market-sourced jewellery first; own/designer collections later.
- A blend of quiet contemporary and modern Indian visual themes.
- English first; multilingual support desired.
- Online checkout plus WhatsApp assistance; no COD.
- Free-delivery threshold to be considered; specialist jewellery logistics desired.
- Short return request window, with refund after return and quality confirmation; exact lawful policy not yet defined.
- Parent-company staff and suppliers handle high-value enquiries.
- Website-first research now; wider business plan separately.
