### Cost calculation — derived, not a vendor quotation

Assuming Shopify's and the gateway's fees apply to the same eligible value, Basic 2% plus a standard 2% gateway charge produces an approximate 4% headline combined rate before applicable taxes on fees. At an illustrative ₹15,000 eligible transaction value, that is ₹600 before applicable fee taxes. Actual billing bases and exclusions can differ; this is not the customer's product GST calculation.

Annual-billing plan comparison: `(5599 - 1499) / (0.02 - 0.01) = ₹410,000` eligible monthly transaction volume. Above this simplified break-even, the 1 percentage-point fee reduction can offset the ₹4,100 subscription difference. With monthly-billing subscriptions the analogous threshold is `(7447 - 1994) / 0.01 = ₹545,300`.

These calculations exclude taxes, additional features, negotiated gateway rates and noneligible transactions. Staff-account requirements may determine the plan independently. The CSV model makes these assumptions editable. Current promotions and credits are excluded.

## Hallmarking and disclosure verification

The initially attempted FAQ URL `https://www.bis.gov.in/hallmarking-overview/faq-hallmarking/` returned HTTP 403. The [BIS homepage](https://www.bis.gov.in/?lang=en) and [hallmarking overview](https://www.bis.gov.in/hallmarking-overview/) were accessible through alternate official paths. The overview exposes links to regulations, mandatory orders, exemptions and jeweller-registration material. The homepage navigation includes a regulation amendment labelled 14 September 2026.

An overview or navigation label does not establish which rule applies to an individual plated-silver or approximately one-gram gold item. Current applicability, exemptions, registration, HUID/invoice requirements and supplier documentation remain **uncertain — needs source and item-specific review**. Do not infer that every SKU must or can carry the same hallmark, or publish a blanket BIS-certified claim.

The website therefore needs separate material, purity, weight and documentation fields per SKU. The parent jeweller should supply evidence, and an appropriately qualified Indian adviser should confirm current obligations before launch. Return-policy and privacy-law compliance also require review; no legal rule or statutory time limit has been asserted in this kit.
